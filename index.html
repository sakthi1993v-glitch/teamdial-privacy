TeamDial Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 October 2026 · App: TeamDial (com.teamdial.app) · Developer: V Sakthivenkadesh

TeamDial is a calling and lead-management app for sales and support teams. A manager creates a company workspace and invites staff; staff call leads from the app and the workspace records call activity so the manager can review it. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who sees it, and how you can have it deleted.

1. Two ways to use the app

  • Team workspace (Manager / Staff). You sign in to a company workspace. Data described in section 2 is stored on our cloud backend and is visible to the people in that workspace according to their role.
  • Personal call history. Optional and requires no sign-in. It reads this phone's call log and shows it on the device only. This feature does not upload your call log anywhere.

2. Data we collect in a team workspace

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Call log (READ_CALL_LOG): matching calls to leads and showing Personal call history. Optional for Personal use.
  • Phone / phone numbers: SIM identification as above.
  • Make phone calls (CALL_PHONE): place the call when you tap a lead.
  • Notifications: follow-up reminders.
  • Internet / network state: sync with the workspace.

You can revoke any permission in Android Settings. Features that depend on it will stop working.

4. Who can see your data

  • Inside a workspace, managers can see workspace leads, staff call activity, attendance and (if enabled) attached recordings. Staff see only their own assigned work.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication and file storage), an email delivery provider (one-time sign-in codes) and Razorpay (payments for company subscriptions).
  • We may disclose data if required by law.

For workspace data, the company that runs the workspace decides what is collected and how long it is kept. Its manager is the primary contact for questions about your workplace data.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Attached call recordings are deleted automatically after the company's retention period (30 days by default), or sooner when a deletion request is approved.
  • Leads that a manager deletes are held in a recoverable trash for 7 days, then removed.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and associated data by emailing sakthi1993v@gmail.com from your registered address. We will respond within 30 days. Some records (for example payment records and audit logs) may be kept where the law requires.
  • Personal call history lives only on your phone; uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

TeamDial – how to request deletion of your account and data

This applies to the TeamDial app (com.teamdial.app) by V Sakthivenkadesh.

  1. Send an email to sakthi1993v@gmail.com with the subject TeamDial account deletion request.
  2. Include your company code and staff ID (or the manager email you registered with) so we can find the account. Send it from your registered email address, or ask your company manager to confirm the request.
  3. We confirm the request and complete it within 30 days.

Deleted: your account and sign-in record, name, phone number and email, attendance and break records, feedback form entries you made, and any call recordings you attached.

Kept (if required): payment and billing records and security audit logs, kept as long as the law requires. Lead and call-log records that belong to your company workspace remain with the company, which controls their retention. Deleted workspace leads are recoverable for 7 days and then permanently removed.

You can also ask us to delete only some of your data (for example, your call recordings) without deleting your account, using the same email.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to workspace data is restricted by role and company using database access rules, and sensitive actions are recorded in an audit log. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

TeamDial is a business tool intended for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, notify workspace managers in the app.
